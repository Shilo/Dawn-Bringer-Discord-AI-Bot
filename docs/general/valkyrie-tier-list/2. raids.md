# Valkyrie Tier List - Raids

## Strategy

Raids focus on more single target damage, and oftentimes the timer is the most challenging aspect to beat. In these scenarios, there should be an emphasis on attack, pierce, crit, and damage increase.

## Valkyries

### Aurora — 🆓

Has a very powerful passive skill that increases back-row Valkyrie damage %. It is currently considered one of the most powerful skills in the game for this reason.

### Madison — 🆓

Has one of the fastest rates of fire in the game, allowing her to trigger pierce and life recovery more often. Her passive also provides a teamwide pierce buff.

### Flame — 💳

Not only does she have a powerful auto-attack due to its area of effect, but her skill provides a teamwide damage % increase.

### YuSheng — 💳

Has a very strong passive teamwide stat boost. Her skills hit multiple times and have a large area of effect, but her short range and slow projectile speed are a disadvantage when the enemy is further away.

### Wendy — 💳

She is a selfish Valkyrie in that her passive skill only affects herself, but her auto-attack and skill are very powerful. Her weapon is what makes her a top-tier DPS due to its damage over time effect, and if the weapon is 5-star and above it shreds 10% defense.

### Misty — 💳

Misty is a great DPS and has unique area of effect with how her auto attack splits. Her skill does loads of damage but has a slow cast time, a long cooldown, and a small area of effect. She pushes raids very well, but lacks in other scenarios.

### Diva — 🆓

Ranked high due to how accessible she is (the only UR obtainable through Valkyrie tickets). She will be the strongest DPS for most, and her weapon further increases her damage output while allowing her to shoot multiple targets making her even better in Corridor/Simulation.

### Alicia — 💳

Alicia has a high rate of fire and a self Attack boost. Her immortality is more of a gimmick because of its short duration, but is nice to have in a pinch.

### Emilius — 💳

Similar to Madison, her passive skill increases back-row pierce and pierce damage. Note: Due to her long range and fast rate of fire, she can stunlock bosses out of range.

### Anna — 💳

Anna is capable of dishing out large amounts of damage and applies a powerful stacking defense shred on the enemy. This makes her very strong for Intercept Supply raid. Unfortunately, her skill has a very short range (like YuSheng) and doesn't trigger when the enemy is far away.

### Kiki — 🆓

5-star attainable from Bio-Beast rewards and has a powerful passive that increases Attack %. Her skill fires randomly and is sometimes unreliable, and she gets outclassed by better Valkyries like Aurora and Madison at later levels. She is still top-tier for F2P due to her passive and attainability.

### Chika — 🆓

Chinatsu Shiraishi has one of the highest single-damage skills in the game, dealing massive area damage while her passive skill boosts normal attack crit damage.

### Milena — 💳

Milena's gimmick of rooting enemies isn't consistent enough to really have a place in any game mode, but I've noticed that it can sometimes help prevent bosses from being knocked back. Her attacks are very slow, and her area of effect leaves much to be desired.
