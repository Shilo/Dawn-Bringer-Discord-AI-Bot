# :handshake: [Newcomer Invitation `(Recruit A Friend)`](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1456044091262369872/1456044091262369872)
> :warning:
> **This system doesn't work like most "Recruit a friend" systems.
> Practically only 1 way rewards.
> Only one bind per player.**

## Recommended
- **Only** mutually (two-way) share and bind codes with one player!
  - **Verify** that they have **not shared/invited code** with others.
- **Do not** bind to anyone posting their code publicly.
- **Do not** share code publicly (for fair play).
__Otherwise, you or another may likely lose rewards.__
> ⚠ Two players should agree and two-way share their codes privately with each other.
> Only one bind per player.
> -# (See  ["Share Issue"](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1456044091262369872/1456122146043461794) below)

## Instructions
1. Find one player who hasn't **binded** `(required)` nor **invited** `(recommended)`.
  - Must do both steps below - `3.` **Share/invite your code** and `4.` **Bind player's code**.
2. Go to `PROFILE > COMMUNITY > NEWCOMER INVITATION` screen.
3. **Share/invite your code** (as Invite__r__ / Refere__r__ / Sharer / Host)
  - Go to `INVITE NEWCOMERS` tab.
  - Copy `MY INVITE CODE` and **share code** privately to other player.
  - **[Progressive rewards](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1456044091262369872/1456122099105009746)** - Invite__e__ level and vip progress (retroactive and prospective).
  - **[One time rewards (small)](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1456044091262369872/1456122099105009746)** - Invite__e__ first bind.
4. **Bind player's code** (as Invite__e__ / Refere__e__ / Binder / Follower):
  - Go to `BIND NEWCOMERS` tab.
  - **Type code** and **bind** with other player.
  - ⚠ __**No progressive rewards.**__
  - **[One time rewards (small)](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1456044091262369872/1456122099105009746)**.
5. Spend **Bond of Friendship** tickets in [Friendship Shop](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1456044091262369872/1456122604157927484).
  - **Recommend**: Buy `50` [**Proof of Friendship**](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1456044091262369872/1456122604157927484) Collection shards for `50,000` **Bond of Friendship**.
      - Within **2.5 weeks** minimum.
      - Only can purchase `20` amount per week.
      - Your **Bond of Friendship** income depends on the progress of who you bind with.

> **⚠ Invite__r__ gets [far more rewards](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1456044091262369872/1456122099105009746), its important that you both mutually share codes.
> So that you both get fair rewards.
> Only one bind per player.**

## [Newcomer Invitation - Share Issue](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1456044091262369872/1456122146043461794)   [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1456044091262369872/1456044091262369872)
> **Don't bind with a public code!**
It's technically possible to have multiple players bind to your code, but it's **greedy**:
- Players (**invite__r__s**) are **publicly sharing** their "invite code", which gives them **multiple rewards** for **each binded player**.
- Other players (**invite__e__s**) that bind with that code only get a **very small reward** and can **no longer bind** with others.
- The **invite__r__** indirectly intentionally or unintentially **steals rewards** from others who have not **mutually binded** with someone.
- **For fair play**, sharing codes should be [mutual (two-way)](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1456044091262369872/1456044091262369872) to earn similar rewards.
