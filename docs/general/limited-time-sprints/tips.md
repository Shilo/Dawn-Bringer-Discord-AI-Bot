# Sprint Tips and Advice

## Currency Management

Unless you're a new player, save your currencies for Sprints, at least enough to finish one round of tasks. You'll receive the tasks rewards upon completion even if you can't get high ranks.

If you have the patience, it's also recommended to be conservative when spending currencies that are not related to known Sprints. We don't have an official schedule and there is chance there might be a related Sprint sooner than later.

## Ranking Strategy

When trying for top ranks, hold on until near the end for pushing for higher ranks if your server is competitive. Note Sprints end 60 seconds before daily reset.
