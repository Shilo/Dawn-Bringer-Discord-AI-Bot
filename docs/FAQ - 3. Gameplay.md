## :heart: [How Do I Enhance My __Survivability__ on Some __Stages__?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> **Knockback**, **Stun** and  **Dodge** substats on gear can be very beneficial for minimizing damage done to your team.
> 
> **[Higher Stage General Strategy]**
> - Pick a side of the field to attack from. 
> - When dangerous enemies get close enough to attack, swipe your team to the other side of the field. 
> - Repeat to delay damage to your team and allow Valkyries to recover damage taken.
> 
> **[Ranged Enemy Strategy]**
> - Ranged enemy shots can be dodged by **rapidly** swiping your team left and right
> - Avoiding ranged damage is important as you advance to higher stages. 
> 
> **[Even Stage XX-01 Strategy:]**
> Breakers (bat enemies) deal piles of damage, but the really brutal enemies are the Spears (the ones that impale from below).
> - When Spears spawn, rapidly swipe your team back & forth to dodge their impale attack.
> - Get the Crit buff by tightly swiping your back & forth in that area.
> - Go back to wide back & forth sweeps to avoid both the Breakers and Spears.
> - Consider using a mix of Offensive & Defensive Collections for **DPS & Survivability**
>

## :test_tube: [Any Tips for __Lab Raid__?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> Clear the Lab Raid is **very punishing** for **early** players, then becomes trivial when Stun nodes in the backpack are unlocked.
> 
> **[Early Advice]**
> **Knockback** & **Stun** substat on gear can be beneficial for early Lab progression.
> - Pick a side of the field (left or right) and keep your team there. 
> - When the AoE puddles come out, go to the other side of the field.
> - On the next set of AoE puddles, go to the middle of the field.
> - Repeat to avoid AoE puddles.
> 
> Avoid the green wave as best as you can by quickly swiping your team out of it.
> - Sometimes, he sends the wave out early and wipes you, sometimes it is delayed. 
> - :beetle: Sometimes you take damage even if you avoid the wave
> - RNG is rough and just try again.
> 
> **[Once You Have Stun]**
> Lab becomes the easiest raid and will be the first one you cap at Lab Level 200.
> 
> DPS Requirements Comparison for Raids:
> [Most to Least DPS Needed]
> Train > Foundry > Supply > Vault > Divine Core Lab >>> **__Lab__**

## :chart_with_upwards_trend: [How is __Damage__ Calculated for __Bio-Beast__?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> Damage done in Bio-Beast is calculated using the host's stats. It applies the host's overall power to all participants.
> - this means that all participants will see differing damage numbers at the end.
> 
> Example 1:
> - Host is 3B power, mercs/allies are 1B power. 
> - Damage Screen will show as if all 3 are using the 3b power's host stats.
> 
> Example 2:
> - Host is 1B power, merc/allies are 400M and 3B power. 
>  - Damage Screen will show as if all 3 are using the 1b power's host stats.
>  
> 🤝 Credit: `@Chaosbutter`

## :abacus: [How is __Damage__ Calculated for __Strongest Team__?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> Just like regular Bio-Beast, damage done is calculated using the host's stats. It applies the host's overall power to all participants.
>

> - Will not redeem Mining Reward Chests.
> - Will not use Mining Reward Drills.

## :crossed_swords: [Any Tips for __Dimensional Corridor__?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> Dimensional Corridor is one of the most, if not the most, difficult game modes currently available. 
> It is an **extreme DPS & Survivability check** and it is **__normal__** to be walled at certain high stages for a week +
> 
> **__Sometimes, you will just not have the stats to progress, no matter what you change or optimize.__**
> 
> **[General Tips]**
> Corridor features 5v5 matches every other stage. 
> - Use a mix of Offensive & Defensive Collections for **DPS & Survivability**
> - Use skills that **Stun** or **Disarm**, such as Precision Strike (has unlisted stun effect) & Heavenly Laser, respectively.
> - **Stun** substats on gear can be very beneficial combined with Stun from backpack. 
> - Experiment with different timer speed modes [1x, 2x, 4x] to see if that helps progression.
> 
> :arrow_right: [What are the __Major Walls__ in __Dimensional Corridor__?]() for specific stage tips.

## :triangular_flag_on_post: [What are the __Major Walls__ in __Dimensional Corridor__?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> The first wall many users encounter is **14-40.** This is when Corridor difficulty really hits hard. Retrying wall stages countless times is **normal.**
> 
> It is **__HIGHLY RECOMMENDED__** (even __mandatory__ at high levels) to run these skills:
>   - **Precision Strike** for it's unlisted stun effect. 
>     - It will **always** target the highest health target, so it is fantastic on Yonai & great for all other stages.
>   - **Heavenly Laser** to prevent outgoing damage via its **Disarm**.
>   
> Generally, a sub 20-25% try means it is numerically possible to beat these stages.
> 
> **[XX-40 Advice]**
> - Goal: **Stunning Yonai** then **burning** her team down. 
>   - Start at **1x speed** to **maximize** the chance of her being stunned.
>   - **Manually** use **Precision Strike** at ~4 seconds into the fight, as this is when Yonai's tornado comes out. 
>   - Adjust speed to [2x or 4x] after this as desired.
> 
> **[XX-34 Advice]**
> This is the stage with 4x Sophia and Stellar Pursuit Dawnbringer.
> - Not as brutal as XX-32 or XX-40, but becomes increasingly difficult in higher levels. 
>   - Going from 100% to dead is **common** when their skills start coming out.
>   - This is a Crit focused team, so consider running **__Crit Resistance__** collections.
>   - Experiment with different timer speed modes [1x, 2x, 4x] to see if that helps progression
> 
> **[XX-32 Advice]**
> Brutal stage with x4 Poposha (Purple Ninja Bunny) & Stormshock Dawnbringer.
> - Passing this stage at higher levels requires lucky **__stuns__**, **__pierce__**, and **__dodges__**.
> - Experiment with different timer speed modes [1x, 2x, 4x] to see if that helps progression
>
