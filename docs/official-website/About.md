# Run! Goddess - Parkour & Shooting Game

Set in an apocalyptic world, it blends anime visuals with runner-style action, offering a mobile experience with strategic progression and real-time controls.

## Game Overview

Players will assume the role of a mysterious female commander, code-named the **Dawn Bringer**, leading a unique Valkyrie Squad through a perilous post-apocalyptic world in a fight for survival.

### Basic Controls

- Tap or swipe to avoid dynamic obstacles such as spiked rollers, flame traps, and surprise attacks by the Infected.

### Real-Time Combat

- While running, use firearms to attack enemies, destroy supply barrels in the scene, and pass through enhancement barriers to gain various buffs and skills.

### Daily Dungeons

- Venture deep into the heart of The Infected Empire, defeat enemies, and rescue survivors.

### Dimensional Corridor

- A challenging roguelike mode where accumulated completion points can be exchanged for SP Valkyrie and Valkyrie Shard rewards.

## Iconic Characters

The game features a diverse cast of powerful Valkyries, each with unique abilities and designs.

## Key Features

- **Fluid action & anime-inspired aesthetics**
- Fast-paced parkour and shooting gameplay
- Strategic progression system
- Real-time combat mechanics
- Diverse character roster
- Post-apocalyptic setting
- Mobile-optimized experience
