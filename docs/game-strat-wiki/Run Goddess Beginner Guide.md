# Run Goddess Beginner Guide Wiki – Cheats & Strategy

Looking for a fun idle shooter that actually delivers on those flashy "fake ad" promises? Run! Goddess is a surprisingly deep game that combines classic idle mechanics, equipment grinding, and fast-paced shooter gameplay.

Whether you're just getting started or aiming to power up your Valkyries, this Run Goddess Beginner Guide covers everything you need to know — tips, tricks, cheats, and strategies that'll help you rise through the ranks with ease.

## Use Redemption Codes for Free Rewards

Head to the **Settings menu** and look for the **Redemption Code** section. Developers often share codes during special events or updates, so keep an eye on official social media channels or Discord communities.

**Tip:** Common redemption codes from other mobile games might not work here, but it doesn't hurt to try!

## How to Get More Armory Boxes (Gear Summons)

Armory Boxes are your gateway to better equipment. Here's how to farm them effectively:

- **Complete quests** (bottom left corner) — you'll earn Armory Boxes, skill tickets, and more.
- **Do Supply Raids** — Use "Raid" on lower difficulties for instant rewards.
- **Challenge Mode** — Fight waves of enemies and bosses to win gear and unlock more game modes.

**Bonus Tip:** Turn on **Auto Mode** to let your characters use skills automatically during battles.

## Understand Equipment Rarity and Upgrades

Gear comes in tiers:

- Green: Uncommon
- Blue: Rare
- Purple: Epic
- Gold: Legendary
- Higher tiers: Mystic → Extraordinary → Divine

Go to the **Armory Box Draw Upgrade** section to increase your odds of summoning higher-rarity items. These upgrades take time, so **start them before going to bed**.

**Pro Tip:** Join an alliance so teammates can help you speed up armory box upgrades.

## Skill System – Don't Neglect This

Go to the **Skills section** to draw new skills. The more you draw, the higher your skill draw level becomes.

- Use all **free skill draws** daily.
- **Quick Upgrade** and **Quick Equip** are your friends — they auto-optimize your Valkyries.

## Recruiting Valkyries and Leveling Them Up

Recruit new characters using **recruitment tickets**. You can:

- Use **Quick Flip** to speed up the process.
- Get characters like **Kiki** and earn universal shards.
- **Star Upgrade** your Valkyries using character shards or universal shards. This significantly boosts their stats and level cap.

## Promotion Quests – Unlock Class Upgrades

Tap on a Valkyrie and complete **Promote Quests**. Once all 3 quests are done, you can:

- Promote them to a higher class.
- Unlock a new title.
- Increase the level cap for all Valkyries.

## Master the Corridor and Challenge Modes

These are level-based combat stages that either:

- Auto-play for idle rewards.
- Require real-time dodging and shooting (especially during boss fights).

**Tips for Boss Fights:**

- Watch the **attack wind-up**.
- Move to the **opposite side** just before the attack lands.
- Prioritize your strongest Valkyrie for boss targeting.

## Use Idle Rewards Effectively

You can claim **2 hours of idle rewards** up to **3 times per day** by watching ads. These rewards include:

- Valkyrie EXP
- Skill medals
- Extra equipment

Don't skip this — it adds up quickly.

## Camp & Crafting System

In the **Camp**, you can:

- Craft new gear (major stat boosts).
- Disassemble old equipment.
- Compose and decompose materials.

**Note:** Some camp features unlock as you progress.

## Unlock & Rescue Valkyrie Aurora

Found in the **bottom-right corner**, Valkyrie Aurora requires **antidotes** to unlock. Ignore the timer — if it expires, it just resets. She'll stay available until you free her.

## Red Dot = Free Stuff

Whenever you see a **red dot**, it usually means:

- You have free items to claim.
- There's an upgrade or reward waiting.
- Mail rewards, quest completions, shop freebies — claim it all!

## Events: Lucky Shooter

In the **Lucky Shooter** event:

- Complete stages or use Armory Boxes to earn **point dice**.
- Higher dice rolls = more multiplier points.
- The more points you collect, the better the rewards you earn.

This is a limited-time event, so **push through stages aggressively** to max out your dice gains.

## Bonus Tips & Cheats

- **Fast-track upgrades:** Set upgrades before bed or during downtime.
- **Auto mode:** Keep it on unless you're struggling with manual skill timing.
- **Manual dodging:** Against bosses, dodge manually for better survival.
- **Level gate tip:** If you're stuck, focus on:
  - Gear upgrades
  - Skill boosts
  - Star upgrades
  - Completing side quests or corridor stages for loot
