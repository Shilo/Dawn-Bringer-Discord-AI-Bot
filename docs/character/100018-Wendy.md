# Wendy - Wolf Alert

> On Curious Night, the Bunny Squad is hosting a daring challenge. According to recent rumors, if you place candy at your doorstep and recite a specific incantation, you can summon a terrifying werewolf from the depths of hell. To test the validity of this rumor, the Dawnbringer dons a vampire costume, just in case a werewolf actually shows up, at least she can scare it off with her presence! Well, it should be helpful... right?

## Skills

### Cursed Pumpkin Shot

Launches pumpkin cannonballs at the enemy with the lowest HP, dealing `[Percent]`% of ATK damage.

### Candied Pumpkin Bomb

Randomly fires 4 rounds of pumpkin missiles at enemies within range, each dealing `[Percent]`% ATK as area damage.

### Wolf Bloodline

Increases skill critical rate by `[Percent]`%, but reduces skill crit damage by `[Percent]`%.

### Team Synergy

Inherits `[Percent]`% of Dawn Bringer’s HP, ATK, and DEF, and enhances `Life Steal` effect by `[Percent]`%

- **✰✰✰✰✰** When Wendy casts a skill, it applies a Corrosion effect that deals additional damage equal to `[Percent]`% of the enemy’s current HP per second for `[Number]` seconds.
