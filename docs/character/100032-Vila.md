# Vila - Vengeful Flash

> Vila is a mysterious Valkyrie clad in face-concealing armor. Wielding orbiting blades, she strikes with deadly precision. Guided by a fierce sense of justice, she fights against the Infected across the wasteland while carrying a past shrouded in secrets. No one has ever seen her true face, and her hidden identity and her overwhelming prowess have made her a living legend.
