# Hoshimi Oozora - Sharp-Eyed Elegance

> A Valkyrie with long pink-and-blue gradient hair and dazzling starry eyes. The vivid patterns on her bodysuit perfectly complement a persona that's innocent yet dangerously unpredictable. To her, the brutal battlefield is just a playground. Wielding a sniper rifle covered in cute cartoon stickers, she fights as if playing a VR shooter, forever chasing her next "high score" and "rank." Rainbow-colored rounds paint the field in cruel brilliance, while the shimmering afterimages left by her shots mirror her elusive, game-like way of life.

## Skills

### Shadow Hunt Snipe

Locks onto the farthest enemy and fires a bullet, dealing `[Percent]`% ATK damage to all enemies along its path.

### Aerial Annihilation

Summons `[Number]` floating sniper rifles that, together with the original rifle, randomly lock onto multiple enemies and fire deadly sniper rounds, dealing `[Percent]`% ATK damage to each target hit. After a 1-second delay, the rounds detonate, dealing an additional `[Percent]`% ATK area damage.

### Lethal Shot

Increases Crit Rate by `[Percent]`%. Each time a Critical Hit is triggered, summons a floating sniper rifle to fire an extra shot at the target that took the critical hit, dealing `[Percent]`% ATK damage.

### Absolute Lock

Increases HP, ATK, and DEF by `[Percent]`%, and enhances `Life Steal` effect by `[Percent]`%
