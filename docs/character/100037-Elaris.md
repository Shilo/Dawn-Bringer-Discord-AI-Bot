# Elaris - Crystal Core Witch

> Elaris, her very name embodies mystery and grace. It is hard to imagine how energy drawn from ancient elven tomes and crystals could be fused so exquisitely with modern technology by her hands.

## Skills

### Hovering Crystal Core

Fires `[Number]` crystal cores at random target locations, each dealing `[Percent]`% ATK damage in a small area.

### Crystallized Fission

Locks onto a target, dealing `[Percent]`% ATK damage and crystallizing them for `[Seconds]`s. When crystallization ends, the target explodes, dealing `[Percent]`% ATK damage in a large area and becomes crystallized again for `[Seconds]`s.

### Crystal Core Shield

When taking any damage, releases crystal core energy outward, dealing `[Percent]`% ATK damage to nearby targets and has a `[Percent]`% chance to crystallize them for `[Seconds]`s. This effect can only trigger once every 6 seconds.

### Crystal Core Power

Increases HP, ATK, and DEF by `[Percent]`%, and enhances `Life Steal` effect by `[Percent]`%
