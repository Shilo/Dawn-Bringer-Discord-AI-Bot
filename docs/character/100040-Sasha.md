# Sasha - Rapid Pulse

> A celebrity coach whose private sessions are notoriously hard to book. She harshly criticizes trainees who are late. During breaks, she's always doing push-ups or drinking protein shakes. Though often rude to employers, she takes her contracts very seriously.
