# Liz - Mech Sakura

> In the fourth year after the Infected catastrophe, the young mechanical prodigy Liz became a Valkyrie. Though she lacked combat prowess, she assembled a humanoid machine named Hirona and wrote an intelligent program for it. During assembly, a mysterious shard accidentally embedded itself in Hirona’s core, granting her true wisdom and emotion.
