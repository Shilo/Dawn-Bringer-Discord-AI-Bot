# Ophelia - Iron Lily

> A discarded mechanical Valkyrie that fell from Sky City—the domain of the Celestials.

## Skills

### Laser Beam

Ophelia fires a laser beam forward, sweeping through all enemies in a straight line and dealing `[Percent]`% ATK damage once.

### Celestial Wing Charge

Ophelia brings her wing-like appendages forward and charges ahead, dealing `[Percent]`% ATK damage to enemies in her path and knocking them back. Ophelia also gains a temporary shield equal to `[Percent]`% of her HP for 8 seconds.

### Celestial Wing Shelter

Each time Ophelia uses Celestial Wing Charge, she grants a temporary shield equal to `[Percent]`% of Max HP to the team for 8 seconds.

### Counter Barrier

Increases Damage Reflection Chance by `[Percent]`%.
