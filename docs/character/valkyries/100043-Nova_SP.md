# Nova - Holy Arrow Judgment (SP)

> On the battlefield, Nova is like a finely tuned tactical instrument, a lone wolf prowling the Infected hives. Her calm composure conceals the fire burning in her heart: behind her goggles, her eyes never waver from the target. Only the sharp whistle of arrows cutting through the air betrays her relentless resolve. A weapon forged for destruction, yet with every precise shot she forces the searing emotions back into her chest.

## Skills

### Holy Arrow Volley

Fires 3 Holy Arrows toward the locked target, each dealing `[Percent]`% ATK as damage to all enemies along its path.

### Alluring Holy Arrow

Blinks to the front of the formation and fires `[Number]` Holy Arrows at random targets. Each arrow deals `[Percent]`% ATK as damage. After casting, the weapon temporarily shifts to the `[Judgment Arbalest]` form for `[Number]` seconds.

### Pursuing Holy Arrow

When Nova deals any damage, there is a `[Percent]`% chance to randomly lock onto `[Number]` enemies and fire additional Holy Arrows. Each arrow deals `[Percent]`% ATK as damage.

### Never Miss

Increases HP, ATK, and DEF by `[Percent]`%, and enhances `Life Steal` effect by `[Percent]`%
