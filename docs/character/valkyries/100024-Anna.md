# Anna - Spoiled Heiress

## Skills

### Lightbeam Whip

Anna swings her Lightbeam Whip, releasing an energy ring that flies forward and deals `[Number]` hits of `[Percent]`% ATK as damage to all enemies in its path.

### Sin Lash

Anna blinks to the front of the formation and lashes the area in front of her multiple times, dealing `[Number]` hits of `[Percent]`% ATK as area damage. Each hit additionally reduces the target's DEF by `[Percent]`%, stacking up to `[Number]` times and lasting for `[Seconds]`s.

### Sin Intensified

Anna’s `[Sin Lash]` skill has a `[Percent]`% chance to knock back enemies and reduce their ATK by `[Percent]`% for `[Seconds]`s.

### Punishment

Increases HP, ATK, and DEF by `[Percent]`%, and enhances `Life Steal` effect by `[Percent]`%
