# Chika Shiraishi - Shadow of the Sun

> Chika Shiraishi, true to her name, is calm and resilient. As Audrey's loyal bodyguard, her devotion runs deep. When the virus outbreak turned the world upside down, she selflessly gave Audrey a chance at life, facing the zombie horde alone. Amidst the gunfire and chaos, she assessed the situation with precision, taking down each approaching enemy. Despite severe injuries, she never lost hope. Miraculously, she fought her way out, carrying scars and a deep concern for Audrey, continuing her solitary journey through the desolate world. She believes that as long as faith remains unshaken, the spark of hope will never die.
