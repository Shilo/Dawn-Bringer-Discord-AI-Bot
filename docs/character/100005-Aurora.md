# Aurora - Dawn Sentinel

> Aurora was once a devout nun who lived peacefully in a remote church. The apocalypse shattered her world. When zombies stormed the church and killed the priest before her eyes, her prayers went unanswered. In despair, Aurora put down her cross and picked up a gun. Her faith transformed into a relentless determination to spread her beliefs in her own way. Her fierce courage in battle makes her fearless, yet it also brings a cold ruthlessness in the face of death. Despite this, she still longs to rediscover the strength of faith and save a world overrun by zombies.
