# Emily - Hand of the Sun

> Emily was a devoted maid to the Audrey family, meticulous and gentle. Before the outbreak, she tended to the mansion and lit the fireplace daily. Now, the once grand estate lies in ruins, and her former masters are gone. She had to adapt, turning her maid uniform into a practical combat suit and picking up a gun to survive. Her resilience is astonishing, as she evolves from a meek servant into a strong, independent warrior.
