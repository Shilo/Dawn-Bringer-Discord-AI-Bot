# SP (Special) Valkyries FAQ

## Is [insert SP Valk here] good?

**No.**

0 ⭐ Valks, even SP rarity, won't outperform a ⭐⭐ or ⭐⭐⭐ SSR or UR.

Given how easy it is to get shards for SSR Valks, SP Valks require insane 💵 to make them viable.

Even a ⭐ SP is not competitive against a ⭐⭐+ SSR or UR.

## Should I spend 💵 to pull [insert SP Valk here]?

**No.**

Banners with pity that include SP Valks are coded such that you will not pull the Valk until you have reached the max pity on the banner, aka 2,000 pulls.

For example, SP Rika on these banners require a $2,000 USD jackpot and you need 192 jackpots to ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ her.

So, she's priced at ~$350,000 USD. Her weapon is ~$90,000 USD to max.

Banners without pity are even worse.

For example, SP Rika averaged $1,000-$1,400 per 10 shards on her pityless banner.

One discord user rolled 2,400 times to get her ONCE.

## But the banner says there is x% to pull [SP Valk]!

This % chance is actually very misleading and Top Games has already gotten in trouble for this very issue.

This is because in reality, the banner is likely coded to be 0% jackpot rate until either the ceiling is met / every single limited item is gone.

In other words, the actual Jackpot rate is 0% until max pity.

Using the SP titan/dragon as an example, the jackpot is listed as 0.05%, which is 1/2000. The ceiling for those banners is 2000 pulls.

You're guaranteed a limited reward every 100 pulls, in which the jackpot is theoretically "increased" to 5%, which is 1/20.

In contrast, 1000 ceiling gatchas are 10% for their limited reward.

Over the 20k+ players in Discord alone, there has not been a single instance in any banner where a user has pulled a jackpot before hitting the ceiling, excluding the "no ceiling" SP banners with Rika, Hecate, and Katherine.

## What about Yonai?

As of 8-8-2025, 0 players have legitimately obtained Yonai.

The closest legit player is at 6B overall power and is at corridor 39-35.

The average player won't reach Yonai in a year. The average whale won't either.

IF you eventually unlock Yonai, and you saved ALL of the Crystal Core Fragments (CCF) given from corridor 1-1 up to that point, you still will not have enough to upgrade Yonai to ⭐⭐⭐.

You get 10 Yonai shards for clearing corridor 40, then another 10 from intimacy with her, so you need another 280 shards or 1.4B CCF for ⭐⭐⭐. For ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ you're looking at 9.5B CCF.

### Spending 💵 to ⭐ her up?

After beating XX-40 stages in corridor, you may be offered a $100 pack that includes 38M CCF.

1 Yonai shard is 5M CCF.

That means every $100 you can buy 7.6 Yonai shards.

A ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Valk takes 1,920 shards total. Subtract the 20 shards from obtaining her & her intimacy, that means you'll need 1,900 total.

1900/7.6 shards per $100 pack = 250 packs needed = $25,000 USD for a maxed Yonai.

Alternatively, 1 CCF = 100 gems, so if you buy 20K gems for $100...

1 shard = (5,000,000 CCF x 100 gem each) / 20k gem packs @ $100 each = $25,000 USD per shard = $47,500,000 USD for max
