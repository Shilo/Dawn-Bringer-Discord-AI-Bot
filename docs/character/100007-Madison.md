# Madison - Fair Judge

> In the post-apocalyptic ruins, survival makes people cautious, but not Madison. As the rebel of the Lightning team, she lives boldly and freely. To her, the apocalypse isn't the start of despair. Madison relishes in others' panic, like a mischievous imp, displaying her flair and madness across the wasteland. You never know what she'll pull from her pocket next. Maybe a deadly weapon, maybe a squawking toy frog. While she can be exasperating, her infectious energy and unique view of fun offer a glimmer of light in the darkness, making her impossible to resist.

## Skills

### Joy Trigger

Rapidly fires bullets forward that can `pierce` enemies, each hit dealing `[Percent]`% of ATK as damage

### Scare Prank

Rapidly fires a large number of bullets forward that can `pierce` enemies, each hit dealing `[Percent]`% of ATK as damage

### Little Surprise

Increases `Pierce` by `[Percent]`% for all characters on the field.

### Team Synergy

Inherits `[Percent]`% of Dawn Bringer’s HP, ATK, and DEF, and enhances `Life Steal` effect by `[Percent]`%
