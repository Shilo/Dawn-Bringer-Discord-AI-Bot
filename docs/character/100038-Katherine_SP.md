# Katherine - Mirage Sea Ballad (SP)

> As the Guardian of the Mirage Sea, Katherine patrols this azure realm with gentle yet unwavering resolve. When the shadow of the Infected Empire threatens to engulf the sea, she draws water like a bowstring and sounds a battle hymn, turning every precisely guided current into a blade of protection. Born in the depths of the Mirage Sea, she is both the keenest edge of dawn and a song of steadfast watch.
