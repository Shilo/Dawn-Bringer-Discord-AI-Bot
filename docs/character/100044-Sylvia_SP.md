# Sylvia - Scarlet Lotus Witch (SP)

> A drifter wrapped in mystery. A symbiotic nanite system resides in her body, capable of a Critical Release that briefly pushes her body and mind into an overdriven adult form. The symbiotic nanites are both her power and her shackle. Her journey under a blood moon is lonely and perilous.

## Skills

### Flameburst Snipe

Locks onto the nearest target and fires a large-caliber piercing bullet, dealing `[Percent]`% ATK damage to all enemies along its path.

### Infernal Queen

Sylvia is instantly engulfed in Blaze, releasing continuous waves of scorching energy. Each wave deals `[Percent]`% ATK damage to surrounding enemies and hurls them backwards. Sylvia then transforms into her `[Infernal Queen]` form for `[Number]` seconds.

### Blaze Splash

When Basic Attacks deal damage, there is a `[Percent]`% chance to launch an extra Blaze at a random target, dealing `[Percent]`% ATK area damage.

### Heart of Flame

Increases HP, ATK, and DEF by `[Percent]`%, and enhances `Life Steal` effect by `[Percent]`%
