# Loranna - Nature's Grace

> As a Floral Whisperer, she tends every bloom in the garden with care. Yet when the Infected bare their claws, she steps onto the battlefield with her parasol, commanding countless plants to surge from the ruins.

## Skills

### Floral Mist

Swings the umbrella toward the nearest enemy to whip up a floral mist, dealing `[Percent]`% ATK damage to all enemies along the path.

### Blossom Grace

Creates a floral mist at the current location, continuously healing allies in a large area around the caster. Heals for `[Percent]`% of ATK every second for `[Number]` seconds.

### Blessing of Blossoms

Allies healed by `[Blossom Grace]` gain `[Percent]`% increased ATK for `[Number]` seconds.

### Ephemeral Bloom

Increases HP, ATK, and DEF by `[Percent]`%, and enhances `Life Steal` effect by `[Percent]`%

## Star Upgrade

- **★✰✰✰✰** Increases own Skill Damage by `[Percent]`%.
- **★★✰✰✰** Increases own Max HP by `[Percent]`%.

- **★★★★✰** Increases own Skill Damage by `[Percent]`%.
- **★★★★★** When dealing any damage, plants an explosive seed in the target. After `[Number]` seconds, the seed detonates, dealing `[Percent]`% ATK area damage to nearby enemies.

## Weapon - Floral Radiance

- **✰✰✰✰✰** Increases Loranna's Skill Damage by `[Percent]`%.
- **★★★★★** Increases Loranna's Skill Damage by `[Percent]`%. The healing effect of `[Blossom Grace]` is increased to `[Percent]`%, and allies healed during `[Blossom Grace]` gain Death Immunity.
