# Mina - Elemental Rhapsody

> Mina is a knowledgeable and captivating Valkyrie, her mind brimming with creativity and wisdom even in the heat of battle. Easygoing in daily life, she sometimes jokes a little too far. Though she may seem unreliable at times, every bit of her technical research is dedicated to the future of both humanity and Valkyries. Her presence brings a touch of humor and intellect to Starport Base.
