> 
> **[Gear Locking Method]**
> - Lock all gear except for Dawnbringer's gear
> - Idle as usual & equip desired upgrades
> - Once Dawnbringer's gear is acceptable, lock that gear
> - Re-equip to the desired Valkyrie using the +
> - Equip a different Valkyrie's undesirable gear to Dawnbringer
> - Repeat until all Valkyries have desired gear

> - Idle as usual
> - When desired equpiment drops, close the game & re-launch
> - Move desired Valkyrie set onto Dawnbringer
> - Upgrade the piece with the new gear drop
> - Move the set back to the desired Valkyrie
> - Repeat for all upgrade drops

> If enough people do the Bargain, the buy price can even **go negative** and give you gems when you **purchase the pack**.
> :arrow_right: [Source](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1346658519683960873/1396958217266794588)
> 
> Imagine its like haggling - Your alliance is trying to get this merchant to give you the best deal possible **before** you pay for the items.
> 
> :exclamation: **Participating in the bargain does not make you purchase the pack!**
> 
> If you want any of the items, you must manually purchase it when you are happy with the price!

## :crossed_swords: [How Does __War Demon Invasion__ Work?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> **[Overview]**
> - War Demon Invasion is an **__Idle boss battle__** where your Dawn Bringer & your Alliance Member's Dawn Bringers fight a boss for Honor Coins, Skill Tickets, and Titan Repair Parts.
>   - Happens 2 times per day, so make sure you are registered
> - Alliance members must Register to participate.
>   - You should **always** Register, regardless of level, for free rewards!
> - At the designated time, a copy of all Registered member's DB will automatically shoot at the Boss. 
>   - This phase lasts 15 minutes.
>   - If you join & leave, you can get a bonus 25 Honor Coins for "participating."
>   - If the group kills the boss, a new boss will spawn.
>   
> **[How to Do More Damage to Boss]**
> - The damage you do to the boss is based on your main **__Stage__** progress and **__Corridor__** progress.
>   - Your level, overall power, Valkyrie lineup, equipped Collections, etc. **__DO NOT IMPACT DAMAGE DONE TO THE BOSS__**.
>   - Want to do more damage? Push Stage & Corridor as high as you can.
>

## :trophy: [How Do __Alliance Duels__ Work?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> **TL;DR** - new game mode, works very poorly right now.
> 
> Players face off aginst another Alliance (or no one if the server is dead) for rewards.
> - Season starts by Matchmaking Phase
> - Players can manually join a lane (top, middle, bottom) during Preparation Phase
>   - After that, all other members will be slotted in randomly
> - Alliance Leaders can make adjustments to the lanes during the Adjustment Phase
>   - Can swap top lane for bottom, etc.
>   - Can adjust player order per lane
>   - Can't swap individual players into a different lane
>   - :beetle: Lane orders are currently locking at the beginning of the season
> - Battles play out during the Battle Phase
>   - First lane defender battles the enemy's first lane defender
>   - Unless the first person is defeated, everyone under them won’t receive any Personal Rewards. 
>   - Personal Points are tied to how many players you defeat.
> - Rewards are distributed after Victory/Loss
>   - All players recieve Win/Loss Rewards
>   - All players recieve Alliance Rewards
>   - Only battle participants recieve Personal Rewards
