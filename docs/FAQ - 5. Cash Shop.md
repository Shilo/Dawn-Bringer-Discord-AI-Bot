## :gem: [Best __F2P Gem Purchases__?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> 1. Idle Reward 2 Hr Earnings (Daily Purchase) - at least up to 3.
>   - 1st: free, 2nd: 100 Gems, 3rd: 200 Gems, 4th: 300 Gems, 5th: 400 Gems
>   - 4th unlocks, at VIP 2, 5th at VIP 8
> 2. Hammers to get Collection to level 5.
> 3. Valk Tickets to get Diva to ★★
> 4. Banner Shop Tickets x20 for 7,500 Gems
> 5. Save around 10-20k Gems for Special Events.
> 6. Keep spending Gems on Hammers for Collections. 
> - Collections are absolutely the best gem:power ratio until Collection Items get to 10 ★
> 
> This is where F2P should generally stop and save, if you want to spend more Gems proceed onward:
> 1. Sprint events, up to EACH STEP worth of completion.
>   - So if you spend Gems, you want to spend enough Gems to complete the step, otherwise save.
> 2. Special Events (usually worth the Gems)
> 3. Freedom Arena extra Tickets (worth it for mining resources you get)
>
