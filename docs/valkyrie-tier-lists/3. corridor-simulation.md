# Valkyrie Tier List - Corridor / Simulation

## Strategy

Corridor and Simulation at higher levels rely on a combination of crowd control, DPS, and survivability. In earlier progression stages, crowd control and DPS are the priority until fights start lasting more than a few seconds. Valkyries that are more area damage focused will rank higher here.

**Invest in:** Global Attack > Stun > Pierce/Crit > Attack

## Valkyries

### Aurora — 🆓

Again, her passive is what makes her top-tier for this content. Her overall damage boost is comparable to 4-star URs.

### Flame — 💳

Flame's area damage helps stunlock a line of enemies in the direction she is targeting. Her % damage increase skill is on-par with Aurora's buff.

### YuSheng — 💳

Has a very strong passive teamwide stat boost. Her skills hit multiple times and have a large area of effect, adding to her crowd control ability. YuSheng does considerable damage to 5v5 layouts like Simulation and Corridor.

### Wendy — 💳

Wendy's skill covers a very large area of effect. Her weapon effect scales with the enemy's stats, which is very powerful due to how the game scales.

### Ophelia — 💳

Ophelia is particularly strong in these game modes due to her auto-attack. The horizontal piercing laser passes through every opponent, leading to more consistent crowd control. Her self and teamwide shield increases survivability.

### Diva — 🆓

Ranked high due to how accessible she is (the only UR obtainable through Valkyrie tickets). She will be the strongest DPS for most, and her weapon further increases her damage output while allowing her to shoot multiple targets making her even better in Corridor/Simulation.

### Emilius — 💳

Similar to Madison, her passive skill increases back-row pierce and pierce damage. Her auto-attacks can stun multiple enemies, adding to her utility in Corridor/Simulation.

### Madison — 🆓

Has one of the fastest rates of fire in the game, allowing her to trigger pierce and life recovery more often. Her passive also provides a teamwide pierce buff. She ranks lower in Corridor/Simulation because of her lack of area effect.

### Misty — 💳

Misty is a great DPS and has unique area of effect with how her auto attack splits. Her skill does loads of damage but has a slow cast time, a long cooldown, and a small area of effect. Can be useful when there are high priority enemies in close proximity to each other.

### Anna — 💳

Anna is capable of dishing out large amounts of damage and applies a powerful stacking defense shred on the enemy. Unfortunately, her skill has a very short range and small area of effect. If the range and AoE was increased, she would easily be an SS tier.

### Milena — 💳

Milena's gimmick of rooting enemies adds no value in Simulation or Corridor. Her defense shred has a very low trigger rate, her attacks are very slow, and her area of effect leaves much to be desired.
