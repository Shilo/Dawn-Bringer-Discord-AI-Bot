# Valkyrie Tier List Guide - Overview

**Author:** YT (US-12)  
**Updated:** 20 July 2025

## Introduction

I've seen many players asking for a Valkyrie tier list, and while there isn't really a definitive answer, a few stand out as noteworthy. Many of the top-tier Valkyries are difficult to obtain as a F2P player.

That being said, there are four F2P-friendly Valkyries that are worth investing in who rank high even among the P2W options:

- **Aurora** (Bio-Beast Frenzy Reward) 🆓
- **Kiki** (Bio-Beast Frenzy Reward) 🆓
- **Madison** (7 Day Log-In Reward & Valkyrie Recruitment Ticket) 🆓
- **Diva** (Valkyrie Recruitment Ticket) 🆓

## Notation

In the descriptions, you will be able to see whether a Valkyrie is F2P or P2W with the following notation:

- 🆓 = F2P (Free to Play)
- 💳 = P2W (Pay to Win)

## Gameplay Categories

There is a ranking for 4 types of gameplay:

1. Raids (Supply, Vault, Foundry, Train, etc.)
2. Corridor/Simulation
3. Main Stages & Other F2P Valkyries
4. Arena

## Assumptions

These lists assume:

- **SR (Blue)** Valkyries are 5-stars.
- **SSR (Purple)** Valkyries are 4-stars or above.
- **UR (Gold)** Valkyries are 3-stars or above.

**SP (Red)** Valkyries have not been included due to the unrealistic investment required for them to be viable (2-stars or above). If you can afford them, you can probably come to your own conclusions on how useful they are.

## General Power Level Comparisons

**SSR = Purple, UR = Gold**

5 Star UR > 4 Star UR > 5 Star SSR ≥ 3 Star UR > 4 Star SSR ≥ 2 Star UR > 3 Star SSR > 2 Star SSR ≥ 1 Star UR > 1 Star SSR

**Note:** Realistically, SRs (Blue) are not worth long-term investment for your main team. (SAVE VALKYRIE XP and SKILL MEDALS)
