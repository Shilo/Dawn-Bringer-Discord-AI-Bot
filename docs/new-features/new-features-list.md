## Ammo Consumption Multipliers
Spend more ammo to get more rewards.

## Corridor Clear Reward Changes
- Copies of YuSheng & her shards available as Floor Clear rewards
- Flame's Valk Costume offered after clearing Corridor Floor 3

## New Corridor Shop Items
Kiki & YuSheng shards now available.

## New Reward UI for Clearing Corridor Stage
Winning any corridor stage gives a battery (at least on lower corridor levels).

## Valkyrie Costumes
Available through corridor progression so far, unknown if it boosts stats.

## Double Valkyrie Barrels in Stages
Mostly present in early stages - you can stack them to get a large number of Valks on the field.

## Valkyrie Factions
Rock / Paper / Scissors mechanic added. Having 3+ of the same faction on your team gives +ATK & +HP.

**Note:** SYNERGY IS NOT FULLY CONSIDERED FOR FACTIONS. For example, Pierce DB is "Shadow" - Madison is "Shadow" but Emilius is "Light" (both being pierce based Valks).

## Dawnbringer Factions
DB Classes also have their associated factions.

## Enemy Factions
Enemies also have their associated Factions.

## Bonus Damage Based on Faction Type
Valks do bonus damage to factions that they "beat" in Rock / Paper / Scissors.

Boosted damage based on faction is shown with ⏫.

This also applies in corridor to enemy valks (even to unreleased valks - Poposha).

## New Freedom Arena View
Cosmetic change making it easier to see.

## Freedom Arena UI Shows Rank Gain
Easy to see how much rank you gain if you beat another player in arena.

## Main Stage Clear Rewards
Get more stuff as you advance in Stage - currently stops at Chapter 47.

## More URs Obtainable With Valkyrie Tickets
You can also pull other UR valks from tickets - Diva is pictured still, but you can receive any of the URs at 100 pulls that are listed.

## Dimensional Arena Open @ Lvl 100
This could just be because the server is so new, but Dimensional arena can't be accessed until level 100.
