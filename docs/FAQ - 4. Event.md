## :information_source: [What is __Lucky Shooter__?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> Lucky shooter is a **__PvP Leaderboard__** game mode where you shoot Infected on the idle screen for dice roll based points.
> 
> - At the start of the event, each player is assigned a leaderboard of other players to compete against.
> - After clearing a wave of Infected, there is a chance to trigger a dice roll that will determine points gained from the dice.
>   - As players idle, the leaderboard will update within ~50 realtime points.
> - Rewards are granted after reaching point thresholds.
>   - Higher thresholds grant higher rewards.
> 
> **[Goal]**
> - Have the most points on the leaderboard by the end of the event. 
> - First place gets 1x Titan Modification Kit
>

## :thinking: [How does the __Lucky Shooter__ Leaderboard __Work__?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> Lucky Shooter seems to use the **__ending point totals__** of players from previous days to sort those players into brackets. :exclamation: `Still Needs Testing to Verify`
> - ex. a bracket of players with 10k points, 30k, 50k, etc. 
> 
> Leaderboards are then generated by mixing the brackets of players together.
> - They seem to prioritize spreading big spenders across Leaderboards 
>   - Possibly to encourage buying dice (the worst value in the game)
>   
> Leaderboard points update dynamically based on each player's point progression on their own boards.

> Limited Time Sprints are weekly Leaderboard events that give rewards based on consumption of differing resources. 
> - There are 2 sprints per week:
>   - a long sprint that lasts 7 days.
>   - a short sprint that lasts 3 days.
>   
> - Clearing a round in a sprint gives 5 Sprint Coins :coin: per round, with 4 rounds total. 
> - Placing in the top 20 (for long sprints) or top 10 (for short sprints) on the leaderboard gives Sprint Tickets :tickets: based on rank.

## :slot_machine: [How Does __Jackpot__ & __Limited Rewards__ Work?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> Jackpots on Banners are likely coded to be 0% until **__ALL__** Limited Reward items offered on the banner have been drawn. 
> 
> Despite the probability being listed at 5% for any Jackpot item to be rewarded on a Limited Reward draw:
> 
> :exclamation: There has __never__ been a report in the __history__ of the game's __Discord__ where a user got a Jackpot item __before__ all Limited Items were drawn. :exclamation:
> - (Yes, that is indeed illegal in the EU & Top Games has gotten in trouble for this)
> 
> For Banners with a hard pity rate (1,000 draws or 2,000 draws), those hard numbers are the **maximum** times you will need to draw in order to guarantee the Jackpot. 
> 
> Typical Jackpot Experiences on Banners with 2,000 draw Hard Pity:
> - God RNG will finish the jackpot in 900 :tickets: 
> - Normal RNG is around 1100~1500 :tickets: 
> - Bad RNG is 1600+ :tickets: 
> (for 1,000 draw Banners, 1/2 those numbers)
