# Valkyrie Tier List - Main Stages & Other F2P Valkyries

## Strategy

The Main Stages focus on waves of monsters. Valkyries that perform well in Raids and Corridor will generally perform well in a main stage. I'll go into more detail on the less used Valkyries here, as there is more team diversity that can be played with.

## Valkyries

### Kiki — 🆓

Kiki is primarily used for her passive teamwide attack boost. Her skill can be unpredictable, but strong when it hits. Also ranked higher due to how attainable she is through bio-beast rewards.

### Rina — 🆓

Kind of like Flame without a damage increase skill, her attacks have good area damage up close for large waves of monsters.

### Audrey — 🆓

Audrey's passive is a powerful defensive buff that reduces incoming damage to the front-row by a %. Her auto-attack is a shotgun blast with a fast rate of fire.

### Chika — 🆓

Chinatsu Shiraishi has one of the highest single-damage skills in the game, dealing massive area damage while her passive skill boosts normal attack crit damage.

### Zoe — 🆓

Zoe boosts the team's skill critical damage and has a well-rounded kit, with fast area effect auto-attack and a multi-shot area damage skill.

### Gabrielle — 🆓

Gabrielle's kit is very similar to Zoe's. Her auto-attack is slightly slower but deals more damage, and her passive provides the team with a defense boost. She ranks low because of the marginal effect of defense in the game's current combat calculations.

### Sophia — 🆓

Sophia is designed for single-target damage with a skill that boosts damage against elite enemies, which can help for raids. Unfortunately, with limited spots in a team she is outclassed by Aurora.

### Elia — 🆓

I've included Elia in my non-D rankings because of her auto-attack, which is a cone-shaped area damage with one of the fastest rates of fire. Helpful for large waves or consistent area crowd control on a budget. I don't recommend investing in her too heavily since she will be eventually replaced.
