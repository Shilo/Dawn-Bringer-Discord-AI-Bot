# Miranda - Red Reaper

> Once the star racer of the "Lightning" team, Miranda's world was shattered by the zombie virus. In the chaos, she lost her teammates, but the roar of her motorcycle through the undead reminds everyone of the Rapid Lightning that once dominated the racetrack. In this mad post-apocalyptic world, she fights despair with speed and tears through the silence with fury.
