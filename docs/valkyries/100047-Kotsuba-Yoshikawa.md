# Kotsuba Yoshikawa - Mark of the Scarlet Eye

> On the surface she is a petite, twin-tailed girl in a cute pink dress, yet she swings a colossal railgun as if it weighed nothing. In daily life she is gentle as a rabbit, twisting her hem when she is flustered. Once the shooting starts, she becomes the merciless "Blooming Requiem,” her thunderous barrages painting the sky with fireworks of ruin and flattening anything in her sights. To her, that roar is life in full bloom.
