# Nicole - Doomsday Cop

> Though she looks young, Nicole's aura rivals that of seasoned officers. Strong-willed and optimistic, she carries a deep sense of responsibility. Just a week on the job when the zombie outbreak hit, she stayed at her post, directing traffic and aiding evacuations. Amid the chaos, she patrolled the ruins, protecting survivors with her baton and radio. "Protecting the people is my mission" became her creed. Her courage has inspired many, making her a new beacon of hope.
