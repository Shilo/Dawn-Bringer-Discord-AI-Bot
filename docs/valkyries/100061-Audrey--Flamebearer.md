# Audrey: Flamebearer - Scorching Sun

> A bit naive and sheltered, she is a noble young lady with complicated feelings about her bloodline.

## Skills

### Flame Shield Spray

Raises a shield-shaped flamethrower and unleashes continuous flames, dealing damage equal to `[Percent]`% of ATK to all enemies within range.

### Blazing Shield Strike

Throws a burning shield that bounces between enemies `[Number]` times, each hit dealing damage equal to `[Percent]`% of ATK, and grants a temporary shield equal to `[Percent]`% of max HP for `[Number]` seconds.

### Flame Shield Counter

When casting `[Blazing Shield Strike]`, increases Damage Reflection Chance by `[Percent]`% and Damage Reflection by `[Percent]`%, while dealing `[Percent]`% of ATK as damage to nearby enemies and knocking them back.

### Blazing Guard

Increases HP, ATK, and DEF by `[Percent]`%, and enhances `Life Steal` effect by `[Percent]`%
