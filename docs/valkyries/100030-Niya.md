# Niya - Fairy Tale Witch

> This petite mercenary Valkyrie always wears a mischievous smile. She wields a gigantic cannon in her hands, a weapon taller than most people that she uses for precision strikes against the Infected. Though she has been fighting since childhood, Niya remains optimistic, her grin able to cut through even the dark battlefield haze. Yet her mature confidence and sly wit often catch Dawn Bringer completely off guard.

## Skills
