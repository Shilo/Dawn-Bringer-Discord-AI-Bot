# Haruka Mizuhara - Tidal Watcher

> Haruka Mizuhara is the Valkyrie assigned to the coastal front of the United Front, keeping watch over the Infected’s encroachment upon the seas. She loves the ocean dearly. Though her work is solitary and arduous, she remains ever full of vitality. This contrast, combined with her strong sense of duty, makes her a reassuring Tidal Watcher within the Dawn forces.
