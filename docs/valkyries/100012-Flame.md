# Flame - Singing Phoenix

> Once a dazzling phoenix on stage, Flame's passionate voice and enchanting dance moves transported audiences into a world of dreams and fervor. But when the apocalypse struck, she lost both her stage and the fire that fueled her spirit. She picked up a guitar, strumming a powerful tune, her music igniting flames that seemed to dance with her. No longer the bright phoenix, Flame became the fire dancer of the apocalypse, using her music to kindle hope and banish darkness. Her voice turned into a weapon, combating despair and fear.
