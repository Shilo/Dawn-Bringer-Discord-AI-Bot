# Misty - Phantom Pitcher

> A girl who fights using the latest gravity-based weapons, throwing powerful gravity bombs like a baseball pitcher to crush her enemies.

## Skills

### Phantom Orb

Misty’s Phantom Orb creates an energy projectile and throws it at the target location, dealing `[Percent]`% ATK as area damage.

### High-Density Phantom Orb

Misty's Phantom Orb gradually condenses high-density energy, forming a massive energy projectile and hurling it toward the target location. It deals area damage 10 times, each hit inflicting `[Percent]`% of ATK, and reduces the target's `Pierce` chance by `[Percent]`%, `Damage Reflection` chance by `[Percent]`%, and `Stun` chance by `[Percent]`% for `[Seconds]`s.

### Phantom Split Orb

Misty permanently increases `Pierce` chance by `[Percent]`%. When her Phantom Orb hits, it additionally splits into 3 small energy balls, each dealing `[Percent]`% of ATK as damage along its path.

### Energy Compression

Increases Pierce Damage by `[Percent]`%.
