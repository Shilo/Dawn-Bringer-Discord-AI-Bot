# Rika - Spirit Fox

> On ordinary days, Rika appears petite and delicate, every gesture brimming with charm and vulnerability. Yet, anyone beguiled by this façade has already stepped unknowingly into the Spirit Fox’s snare. Once Rika transforms into her Bewitching Fox form, she turns feral in an instant, radiating a powerful aura. Her agility and razor-sharp strikes often leave enemies with no time to react.
