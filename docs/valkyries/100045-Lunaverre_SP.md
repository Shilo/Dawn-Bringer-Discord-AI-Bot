# Lunaverre - Moon-Veiled Thousand Nights (SP)

> She exudes an elegant touch of menace, moving with the composure of nobility and taking pleasure in teasing her opponents. She blends easily with other Valkyries while maintaining just the right air of mystery. Her words are laced with innuendo and double meanings, mixing truth with lies. Her identity remains shrouded in rumor—some claim she is the sole heir of a noble house—yet no evidence has ever surfaced.
