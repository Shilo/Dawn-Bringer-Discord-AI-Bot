## :globe_with_meridians: [What is a __War Zone__?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> The Warzone is essentially the server number a player is assigned to.
> 
> It can be accessed by: 
> - Tap Player portrait in top left of Main Idle Screen
> - Tap Player portrait again
> - War Zone is shown at the top. 
>   - Tapping the double arrow next to it shows all available War Zones
>   
> :exclamation: You currently cannot transfer an existing character to a new War Zone.

## :link: [How to __Link Account__?](https://#)    [▲Top](https://discord.com/channels/1330797390185234513/1423687297454575716/1423687297454575716)
> The most reliable way to play your current account on other devices is to link it through **Facebook.**
> 
> :beetle: Discord Users have reported issues linking their accounts with Google Play.
> 
> To play your account on other devices:
> - Install the game on new device
> - Play through the tutorial (no way to skip currently)
> - After obtaining Miranda & adding her to lineup, tap Player portrait in top left of Main Idle Screen
> - Tap Player portrait again
> - Tap "Link Account"
> - Tap "Switch Account"
