# Best Builds Guide

*This assumes ANY UR's are TWO Star+*

## Build Rankings

**Pierce Pink > Crit Pink > Blue Stun**

- Pierce pink is slightly less effective than blue stun till about level 100
- Starting with Blue stun then moving to pierce pink at 100 might be more efficient

---

## Pierce Pink - Bottom Pink Class Path

### Top F2P Team
- **Front:** Aurora, Madison
- **Back:** Diva, Kiki, DB

### Top P2W Team
- **Front:** Flame, Aurora 4-5*
- **Back:** DB, Emilius, Diva OR Yu

### Power Level (assuming equal star level)
Aurora 4-5* > Flame > Emi > Aurora > Yu > Diva = Madison > Kiki

**Notes:**
- If Yu is within ONE star of Diva she is VERY close to as efficient
- Flame and Emi are BiS if 2star+

### Recommended Collection Items
Farmers Snake > Lucky Charm > Long Range Goggles > Hooded Girl > Ten Thousand... > Pixel Sword = Water = Mistletoe
