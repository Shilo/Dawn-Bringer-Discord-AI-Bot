# Game Specific Descriptions of Stats

*Credit to @Drummer for the game data dump and @OldManMagee for parsing some of that data to find this information*

## Basic Attack Stats

- **Critical Hit** - Chance for basic attacks to crit
- **Crit Resistance** - Reduce chance of receiving crits from basic attacks
- **Crit Damage** - Increases Crit damage for basic attacks
- **Basic Attack Damage** - Base damage multiplier for basic attacks

## Skill Stats

- **Skill Critical Hit** - Chance for skills to crit (Equipable skills, & Valkyrie second attack)
- **Skill Crit Resistance** - Reduce chance of receiving skill critical hit
- **Skill Crit Damage** - Increases Skill Crit damage
- **Skill Damage** - Base damage multiplier for skills

## Pierce Stats

- **Pierce** - Basic attacks and skills have a chance to penetrate enemy defenses, dealing extra damage
- **Ignore Pierce** - Reduce chance of receiving pierce damage
- **Pierce Factor** - The damage multiplier for pierce attacks, affects overall damage of pierce attacks
- **Pierce Damage Reduction** - Reduces the damage taken from pierce attacks

## Damage Reflection Stats

- **Damage Reflection** - When attacked, there is a chance to deal extra damage to the attacker based on the damage reflection ratio and ATK
- **Ignore Damage Reflection** - Reduces the chance of receiving damage reflection effect
- **Damage Reflection Factor** - The base damage multiplier for damage reflection, affects the damage output of DR effect
- **Damage Reflection Reduction** - Reduces the damage taken from damage reflection effect

## Status Effects

- **Stun** - Basic attacks and skills have chance to stun enemies
- **Knockback** - Basic attacks have chance to knock enemies back

## Recovery Stats

- **Lifesteal Rate** - Chance to recover HP, based on the damage dealt
- **Hit Recovery** - Recover HP after hitting

## Boss and Monster Stats

- **BOSS Damage** - Increases additional damage dealt to Bosses
- **Damage to Monster** - Increases additional damage dealt to monsters
- **Monster Damage Reduction** - Reduces the damage taken from monsters

## Unknown Stats

Unfortunately we still do not know for sure what **"Ratio"** and **"Final Damage"** stats do.
