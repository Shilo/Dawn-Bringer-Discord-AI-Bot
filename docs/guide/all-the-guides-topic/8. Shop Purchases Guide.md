# Shop Purchases Guide

## Sprint Shop

1. **Wings first**
   - Pink for Pink DB
   - Blue for Blue DB

2. **After you have your wings:**
   - Purchase 1 copy of Holy Lance Emblem
   - Purchase 1 copy of the OTHER wings
   - The order doesn't really matter unless you do NOT have "Godzilla Tank"
   - If you don't have Godzilla Tank: get the Holy Lance Emblem next, then the other copy of wings

## Alliance Shop

- **Personally recommended:** Hoard these points
- If you HAD to recommend something: **Cupcakes**
  - Can be used to (slowly) increase your favorite Valk's relationship modifiers
- This is the shop that seems the least useful right now, which is why hoarding points is recommended

## Dimensional Arena Shop

### 1st Priority
**Energy Drink (10,000 points)**
- Red collection item that gives tons of global stats
- Cannot be earned in ANY other way (including using collection hammers)
- **Exception:** If a "Collection Appraisal Sprint" is going on, delay getting the energy drink
  - Buy as many hammers as you can (100 per step, 400 needed total) to get the collection awards for using hammers

### 2nd Priority
- Either one more Energy Drink (to two-star the Energy Drink)
- OR start saving for the World Scorcher (80,000 points)

### 3rd Priority
- Either TWO more Energy Drinks (to 3-star)
- OR Start saving for World Scorcher

### 4th Priority
**World Scorcher**
- Gold Cores are INCREDIBLY rare
- Their odds are listed as 0.14% which means on average you would need 715 draws to get ONE (572,000 gem cost)
- However, based on experience and many people on Discord, cores appear to have even LESS than a 0.14% chance to be drawn in the core gacha
- World Scorcher provides a BASE HP scaling bonus of 675%, before you refine it with core dust

### 5th Priority
After you have World Scorcher and 3-Star Energy Drink (120,000 POINTS total - this will take a LONG time):
- **Tech Crystals**

### Alternative
If saving points forever to get World Scorcher does NOT appeal to you:
- Start spending DA points on either **Tech Crystals** (likely best option) or **Hammers**

## Corridor Shop

*This is very tricky to put values to the different options, but here is the current view:*

### 1st Priority
**Technically:** Saving for Yonai Shards (5mil each)
- However, corridor is such a wall that this would require 5-6 MONTHS or MUCH longer if F2P before you can actually get to 40-40 and unlock Yonai
- As such, it is probably MUCH more realistic to go to step 2

### 2nd Priority (if ignoring Yonai)
**Tech Crystal > Hammers > Drone Parts = Tank Parts > Fiber > Divine Core**

- For drone VS tank parts: buy whatever is scaling LOWER than the other in their respective screens

### Sprint Events
If there is a **SPRINT** going on, whatever that sprint is becomes the highest value:
- Collection sprint = hammers
- Tank sprint = tank parts
- etc.

**Note:** This is all opinion-based and cannot be said to be 100% accurate. This is just based on experience and knowledge of this game.
