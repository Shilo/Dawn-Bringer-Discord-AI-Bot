# Best Dawn Bringer Classes Guide

## Class Rankings

### 1. Pierce Pink (Firepower Vanguard, Bottom Pink) ⭐ **BEST FOR PVE**
- With the recent 0.0.13 patch, crit damage builds have been nerfed fairly hard
- Blue Stun took the nerf the hardest, while Pink Crit is still pretty good
- **Pierce Pink with a piercing based team is by far the strongest current option for PVE**
- Emilius's release only makes pierce builds EVEN stronger

### 2. Crit Pink (Assault Gunner, Top Pink)
- More crit focused, not AOE, piercing
- Appears to have highest sustained damage
- Decent for PVP but doesn't burst like Blue does at start of combat

### 3. Stun Blue (Precision Spark, Top Blue)
- AoE focused and potentially stun focused
- Has the highest burst damage in general and at the start of combat (really good for PVP)
- Best for PVP outside of Red, and still really good for PVE

### 4. Red
- Good for specifically PVP, Corridor, and Digital Simulation
- **Worse than pierce pink for EVERYTHING else**
- Focuses on defense and damage reflection which is NOT good for PVE in general
