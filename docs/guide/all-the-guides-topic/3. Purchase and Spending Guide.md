# Purchase and Spending Guide

## Recommended Purchases (in rough order)

### 1. Annual VIP Card (Sale Price: $29.99)
- **The single most valuable purchase in the entire game**
- Sale price is originally available for 24 hours when you FIRST start the game
- If you are going to spend anything, spend it on this ASAP before it jumps to full price
- Full price: $99.99 (loses its appeal at this price)
- The Annual VIP Card does appear to go on sale periodically
- **Only purchase at the sales price**

### 2. Monthly Card (Sale Price: $1.99)
- **The SECOND most valuable purchase in the entire game**
- At $4.99 price it is STILL the second most valuable purchase

### 3. Special Case: Luxury Privilege + Flame Encounter
- **IF** you buy Luxury Privilege ($19.99) + Flame Encounter 1 ($0.99) **OR** Flame Encounter 2 ($2.99)
- This gets you enough shards for the 2-Star UR Flame which is VERY high value
- **However**, if you do NOT buy these things in combination, then skip ahead

### 4. Flame Encounter 2 ($2.99)
- Solid value all around
- Even if you don't 2-star Flame (though you won't use her late game)

### 5. Monthly Pass ($9.99)

### 6. Growth Fund ($9.99)

### 7. Growth Power ($9.99)

### 8. Luxury Privilege ($19.99)
- Only worth it if you buy Flame Encounter 1 or Flame Encounter 2

### 9. Event Packs ($29.99)
- Usually worth it if you are going for URs

## Additional Spending Guidelines

If you are STILL looking to spend after all of this:
- Anything with **5000% value** is a HUGE deal
- Anything with **2500% value or more** is a great buy
