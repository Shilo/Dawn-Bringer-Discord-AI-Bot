# Curious Night Event

## Overview

In this event, you launch Pumpkin Bombs at an Infected, bringing down its health to obtain rewards, Fun Coins and Fun Points.

### Infected Types

There are 3 types of Infected to bomb:

- **Pumpkin Infected** - Your basic target to attack. Drops rewards when attacked/defeated.
- **Mysterious Pumpkin Infected** - Can be summoned when the normal Pumpkin Infected is killed. It drops better rewards than Pumpkin Infected, but may flee before being defeated.
- **Super Pumpkin Infected** - A boss that can be killed once per day. Once killed, you are entered to win a lucky draw for more rewards - distributed the next Event day.

Use the Arrow Icon next to their health bar to swap between the Regular and Boss Infected.

## How To Play

Complete Challenge Tasks to obtain Fun Points and Pumpkin Bombs.

- **Pumpkin Bombs** are your ammo to attack the Infected.
- **Fun Points** count towards your Leaderboard Ranking Score
  - Rewards the top 100 across every server in your region.
  - There are three brackets: US, International, and Asia
- Every 2 hours, you can claim 10 free Pumpkin Bombs - capped at 5 times per day.

### Combat Mechanics

- You can attack the Infected x1 or x10 with Bombs.
- If the Infected dies before all 10 bombs are used, you will be refunded the unused bombs.
- Each bomb does 5% HP to the normal Infected.
- To the Boss, each bomb does 1%.
- Bombs have a chance to do x2, x3, x5 & x10 damage.
- Every so often the 1 bomb use can be used for free. Make sure to use these as they pop up.
- These free uses can combo into more free uses.
- Auto attack will consume shots x1 at a time and will also consume free shots.

**Strategy:** Use all Bombs on the normal Infected for rewards.

**Note:** Considering how limited bombs are and how much health the Boss has, it is NO LONGER recommended to defeat the Boss daily.

## Curious Night Treasure

**Still being tested to verify this is how it works**

Within your region, as people defeat Infected, the Curious Night Treasure counter increases.

- It counts upwards to 3,000, then resets back down to 0.
- Upon reset, it will award a participant in the 3,000 kills the grand prize of 1,000 pumpkin bombs.

**September Re-run Update:** Counter INCREASED to 10,000

The game states that the more Infected you defeat in the counter, the higher your odds are to win the grand prize.

### Community Findings

- Since the Curious Night Treasure odds apparently increase based on how many of the rare spawn infected (Mysterious Pumpkin Infected) you defeat, it very well may be coded so that whoever beats the most in that kill draw pool might win.
- If there's a tie (say 5 people beat 6 rare spawns), then it draws at random between those.

## Attacking Rewards

The possible rewards from attacking an Infected is as follows:

- [55%] Gems
- [05%] Cupcake
- [15%] Titan Repair Parts
- [15%] Dragon Fighter Parts
- [10%] Magic Crystal

When an Infected is defeated, there is a chance for them to reward Fun Coins.

Additionally, defeating the Boss Infected gives you a 20% chance to get a Magnet Steel Rhino Box.
This box has a low [0.5%] chance to reward the UR Magnet Steel Rhino Titan.

## Challenge Tasks

Each completed Challenge Task progresses the battlepass, with each step rewarding 5 or 25 Pumpkin Bombs.

The challenge quests for this event are generally considered to be the "harder" set to complete:

- Log in
- Clear Corridor 1x & 5x
- Citrine mining 10/20/30/40/50x plots
- Recruit Valkyries 50x
- Draw Skills 210x

The "Participate in Pumpkin Killer every 1 Time" quest is a poorly worded way to say that every time you attack the Infected, you get 2 Fun Points. It is infinite and will not be cleared from your Challenge Task List.

**Battlepass Notes:**
- Not worth spending gems to increase the "purchase level" on the battlepass, as you will clear it just fine in the event.
- Likely not worth the paid Battlepass Upgrades, as they only give a small amount of gems, bombs, and Steel Rhino Boxes (only in the $5 pack)
