Valkyries Guide

UR (Ultra Rare) Valkyries:
- Aurora (Dawn Sentinel): Ranged attacker with high damage output
- Diva (War Rose): Support character with healing abilities
- Flame (Singing Phoenix): Fire-based attacks, good for crowd control
- Wendy (Wolf Alert): Fast melee fighter with high mobility

SP (Special) Valkyries:
- Mina (Elemental Rhapsody): Versatile elemental attacks
- Irene (Midnight Fireworks): Explosive area damage
- Eve & Ashe (Time Rift Twins): Dual character with time manipulation
- Lynn (Dessert Maestro): Unique support abilities
- Ruby: High damage dealer
- Liz (Mech Sakura): Mechanical-themed fighter

Team Composition Tips:
- Balance between ranged and melee Valkyries
- Include at least one support/healer
- Consider elemental synergies
- Higher rarity doesn't always mean better - match to your playstyle
